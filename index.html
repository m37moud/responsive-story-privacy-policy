Privacy Policy

App: Shorouk Alshams for Kids / شروق الشمس للأطفال

Last updated: 2026-05-23

This Privacy Policy explains how Shorouk Alshams for Kids ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use the app.

1. Information the app uses

  • App and device information needed to run on your device, such as Android version and app version.
  • Local app data stored on the device, such as onboarding state, preferences, and downloaded media for offline playback.
  • Notification permission status used for download notifications when offline downloads are enabled.
  • Information you choose to send directly, such as email feedback.

2. How information is used

  • To provide story playback and learning content.
  • To support offline downloads and playback.
  • To remember local settings and app state.
  • To show download-related notifications when needed.
  • To let you contact the developer by email or open optional external links such as Facebook, YouTube, or the Play Store page.

3. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. The app primarily stores its operational data locally on the device. If you choose to open external links such as email, Facebook, YouTube, or the Play Store page, those third-party destinations handle information according to their own privacy policies.

4. Children's privacy

The app is intended for educational use. We aim to keep data handling limited to what is needed for the app to function, including local preferences, offline media, and optional notification behavior for downloads.

5. Data retention

Downloaded media, preferences, and similar app data remain on your device until you remove them or uninstall the app.

6. Your choices

  • You can stop using the app at any time.
  • You can uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data from your device.
  • You can manage notification permission in your device settings.
  • You can choose whether to open external links or contact us by email.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: m37moud00@gmail.com
