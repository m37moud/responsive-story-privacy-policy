Last updated: 2026-05-30

This Privacy Policy explains how Shorouk Alshams for Kids ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use the app.

1. Information the app uses

  • App and device information needed to run on your device, such as Android version and app version.
  • Local app data stored on the device, such as onboarding state and app preferences.
  • Information you choose to send directly, such as email feedback.

2. How information is used

  • To provide story playback and learning content.
  • To remember local settings and app state.
  • To let you contact the developer by email or open the app's Play Store listing.

3. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. The app primarily stores its operational data locally on the device. If you choose to open external actions such as email or the Play Store page, those third-party destinations handle information according to their own privacy policies.

4. Children's privacy

The app is designed for educational use by children and families. We aim to keep data handling limited to what is needed for the app to function, including local preferences and bundled educational media.

5. Data retention

Preferences and similar app data remain on your device until you remove them or uninstall the app.

6. Your choices

  • You can stop using the app at any time.
  • You can uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data from your device.
  • You can manage notification permission in your device settings.
  • You can choose whether to contact us by email or open the app's Play Store page.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: m37moud00@gmail.com
